Phases

Why the Same Week Means Different Things

The 24-month programme runs in five phases, and a practice day in month 2 is not a practice day in month 20. Early on the work is corrective — stop the bleeding, take the double-bogeys out. Later it is about sharpening a game that already works and holding it under pressure. The focus lines on this page shift with the phase you're in.

Months 1–2 · Stop the BleedingRemove the big numbers
Months 3–4 · Build the FoundationRepeatable contact
Months 5–12 · Build the AttackStart scoring, not surviving
Months 13–18 · Elite ExecutionSharpen and convert
Months 19–24 · Defend the StandardHold it under pressure
Honesty

What This Page Can't Know

It doesn't know whether you actually did Tuesday's session, whether your back hurts, or that Saturday's round got rained off. The week it shows is the week you told Quick Start you intended — treat it as a plan to argue with, not a schedule to obey. If the shape is wrong, change it in Quick Start and it will follow.

Handicap milestones are a trajectory, not a promise They scale the same 24-month curve to your own start and goal. Real improvement is lumpy — plateaus and jumps are normal, and being behind the line in month 7 says very little on its own.
